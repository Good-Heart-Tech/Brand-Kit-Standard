# Start your brand kit

This guide is for nonprofit staff. You do not need to be technical. A volunteer
or IT partner can handle the computer parts; your job is to decide what the
brand is.

## What you get

- **One place** for your logo, colors, fonts, how you sound, and your approved wording
- **One page** anyone can open to see the whole brand
- Websites, email signatures, and AI tools that all use the **same** colors and words
- **Automatic checks** that your text colors are easy to read
- A **checklist** that helps stop scammers from pretending to be you

## How long it takes

| Kit size | Time | What it covers |
|----------|------|----------------|
| Minimal | About 2 hours | Colors, logo, readability checks, scam protection checklist |
| Full | One or two afternoons | Everything above, plus who you are, how you sound, and approved wording |

You can start small and add more later.

## Step by step

### 1. Gather what you have (30 minutes)

- Your logo files. Ask your designer for an **SVG** if you can. PNG is fine too.
- Your colors, if you know them (six-character codes like `#2F6B3A`). If not,
  your volunteer can pick them from your logo or website.
- A few things you have written that sound "like you": a newsletter, a donation
  letter, your website's About page.
- Your mission statement and any boilerplate you send to press or funders.

### 2. Fill in the worksheet (1 hour)

Use the [intake worksheet](intake-worksheet.md). Answer in your own words;
there are no wrong answers. Your volunteer turns your answers into the kit.

### 3. Your volunteer sets up the kit

They run `bkr init`, fill in each `TODO(bkr)` section from your worksheet, and
check that everything passes. Then they send you the brand-at-a-glance page.

### 4. Review the brand-at-a-glance page

Open it in any browser. Check:

- Are these the right colors and logos?
- Does every row in the readability table say **Pass**? If something fails, your
  volunteer will suggest a slightly darker or lighter shade.

### 5. Decide what you share

The kit is **private** unless you choose otherwise. Options:

| Setting | Means | Good for |
|---------|-------|----------|
| Private | Nothing leaves your organization | Most organizations |
| Partner | Only chosen files go to partners and sponsors | Co-branded events, sponsors |
| Public | Only chosen files may be shared with anyone | A press kit page |

Logos and colors are safe to share; they are already on your website. Keep
email templates, donation page designs, staff contact lists, and internal names
private. See [Protect your brand](protect-your-brand.md).

### 6. Use it

- Load it into Canva: [Canva guide](canva.md)
- Ask your web person to use the generated CSS file
- Give AI assistants the `AGENTS.md` file from the kit

### 7. Keep it current

When something changes (new logo, new tagline), tell your volunteer. They
update the kit, and every tool that reads it stays in sync.
