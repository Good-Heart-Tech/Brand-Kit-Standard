# Vocabulary

## Prefer

- “team” over “resource”
- “sign in” over “log in” (unless OAuth provider copy differs)
- “email” over “e-mail”

## Avoid

- “synergy”, “disrupt”, “best-in-class”
- “users” when “people” or “admins” is clearer

## Inclusive language

- Gender-neutral defaults
- Avoid idioms that do not translate
