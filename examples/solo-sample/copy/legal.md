# Legal and image usage

This is a plain-language summary for clients. The signed project agreement
always wins if the two disagree.

## Photo and design licensing

- I keep the copyright to every photo and design I make. Clients get a license
  to use them.
- **Standard license (included in every project):** use the final photos and
  designs for your own business, forever, on your website, social media, menus,
  signs, ads, and printed materials.
- **Not included without written permission:**
  - Selling the photos, or giving them to another business to use as its own
  - Using them in a logo or trademark (logo design projects are the exception,
    see below)
  - Stock photo sites, contests, or AI training data sets
- **Logo design projects:** once the final invoice is paid, the client owns the
  logo outright, including the trademark rights.
- **Editing:** cropping and resizing are fine. Please do not add filters or
  change colors on my photos; ask me and I will do it.
- **Credit:** a photo credit ("Photo: Juniper Lane Studio") is appreciated on
  social media but not required.

## People in photos

- I get a signed model release from anyone recognizable before using a photo in
  my own portfolio.
- Clients are responsible for permission from their own staff and customers
  when they share photos.

## My portfolio

I may show finished work in my portfolio and on social media unless the client
asks me not to in writing before the project starts.

## Brand assets in this kit

- The Juniper Lane Studio name, the JL monogram, and the wordmark are mine.
  Printers and vendors may use them only to produce work I ordered. Clients may
  use them only for a photo credit.
- Fonts: Cormorant Garamond and Karla are free under the SIL Open Font License.
  I do not ship font files in this kit; download them from Google Fonts.
- Tooling that reads this kit may be MIT licensed; the brand assets in this kit
  are not.
