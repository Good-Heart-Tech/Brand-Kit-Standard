# Colors

The official values live in `tokens/colors.bkr.json`. Open
`tokens/exports/html/brand-at-a-glance.html` to see them. There is no dark
theme: the studio's website, proposals, and print work are all on light paper.

## Defaults

- Page background: `paper` (warm, like good photo paper)
- Print margins, photo mats, and forms: `white`
- Headings and body text: `charcoal`
- Links: `link` on `paper`, `white`, or `wash`
- Buttons: `onPrimary` label on `primary` (juniper green)
- Cards, pricing boxes, and callouts: `wash`
- Thin rules, small shapes, and photo frames: `blush`

## Let the photos lead

Most of the page is photography. Keep the brand colors quiet around it: paper
backgrounds, charcoal text, and one juniper button per screen or page.

## Do not

- Use `blush` for text, or put white text on `blush` (it is far too light)
- Put `primary` text on `charcoal` (two dark colors)
- Fill whole pages with `primary`; save it for buttons, bands, and the logo
- Add colors that are not in the tokens
