# Style rules

A short checklist, not a style manual. When something is not covered here,
follow AP style and add the decision here.

- **Headings:** Sentence case ("Start a Fresh Drop", with program names kept in Title Case)
- **Coffee names:** Title Case: "Switchback", "First Light". Single origins: origin first, then farm or co-op: "Huila, Finca La Esperanza"
- **Roast levels:** "light", "medium", "dark", lowercase, never internal roast numbers
- **Prices:** Currency symbol, no ".00": "$18", "$17.50"
- **Sizes:** "12 oz bag"
- **Lists:** Oxford comma ("peach, honey, and black tea")
- **Numbers:** Spell out one to nine; numerals for 10 and up, prices, sizes, and percentages
- **Dates:** "October 2, 2026" in prose; roast dates on bags as "Roasted Oct 2, 2026"
- **Times:** "7 a.m." and "2:30 p.m."
- **Person:** "we" for Ridgeline, "you" for the reader, "guests" in the cafes, "partners" for wholesale accounts
- **Exclamation points:** At most one per page or post
- **Emoji:** Only on social media, never on bags, menus, or legal text
- **Links:** Describe where the link goes ("see the brew guide"), never "click here"
