# Sensitive topics

How we talk about subjects where a careless sentence could hurt trust. For each
topic: our position, what to say, what not to say, and who approves anything
public. If a topic says **no comment**, do not write about it at all.

| Topic | Our position | Say | Do not say | Approver |
|-------|--------------|-----|------------|----------|
| Price changes | Be upfront and give notice | The new price, when it starts, and the plain reason (for example green coffee costs) | Blame on farmers or suppliers; "small" or "slight" increase | Owner |
| Wholesale prices and terms | Private; shared only with the account | "Talk to our wholesale team" | Any wholesale price or term in public copy | Wholesale manager |
| Sourcing claims | Only what we can prove | The farm or co-op by name, the region, and how we buy when it is documented | "Organic", "Fair Trade", "direct trade", or "ethically sourced" unless `copy/claims.md` allows it | Owner and head roaster |
| Competitors | We talk about our coffee, not theirs | What makes our coffee fresh and our service dependable | Names of other roasters, cafes, or chains; comparisons or criticism | Owner |
| Late or wrong orders | Own it and fix it | Sorry, what we are doing, and that the customer does not need to prove anything | Excuses or blame on the shipping company | Online shop manager |
| New locations | No comment until announced | | Anything about a third cafe or tasting room | Owner |
| Politics | No comment | | Any position on candidates, parties, or elections | Owner |
