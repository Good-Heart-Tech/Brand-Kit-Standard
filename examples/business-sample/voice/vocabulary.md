# Vocabulary

## Prefer

- "fresh-roasted" with a roast date over "premium" or "gourmet"
- "tasting notes" over "flavor profile"
- "guests" (in the cafes), "you" (online), and "partners" (wholesale accounts)
- "farm" or "co-op" by name over "sourced from around the world"
- "light, medium, dark" over internal roast numbers
- "brew guide" over "instructions"

## Avoid

- Snob words: "real coffee", "proper espresso", "you are doing it wrong"
- Hype: "best coffee in the world", "life-changing", "artisanal" on everything
- Unproven claims: "ethically sourced" or "direct trade" unless we can name the
  farm and the terms (see `copy/legal.md`)
- Health claims of any kind
- Jargon without a quick explanation: "extraction", "TDS", "washed process"

## Spelling and style

- "pour-over", "cold brew" (no hyphen), "espresso" (never "expresso")
- Coffee names in Title Case: "Switchback", "First Light"
- Prices with the currency symbol and no ".00": "$18", "$17.50"
- Gender-neutral defaults and short sentences that work on a phone screen
