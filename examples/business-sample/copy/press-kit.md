# Ridgeline Coffee Roasters press kit

**Boilerplate:** Ridgeline Coffee Roasters is a small-batch specialty coffee
roaster founded in 2014. It roasts coffee from named farms and co-ops, runs two
neighborhood cafes, ships fresh coffee nationwide through its online shop, and
supplies local restaurants, offices, and grocers.

**Name:** Ridgeline Coffee Roasters on first mention, then "Ridgeline."
Please do not shorten it to "RCR."

**Cafes:** Ridgeline Downtown and Ridgeline North End.

**Logo:** Use the files in `assets/logo/` as they are. Use `mark-reversed.svg`
or `wordmark-reversed.svg` on dark photos. Do not recolor, stretch, or add effects. See `visual/logo.md`.

**Colors:** Espresso, crema, and copper. Exact values are on the
brand-at-a-glance page included with this kit.

**Photos:** Ask us for current cafe and roastery photos. Please do not use
photos of our staff from social media without asking.

**Media questions:** use the contact page on our website
(https://ridgelinecoffee.example/contact) and choose "Press."
