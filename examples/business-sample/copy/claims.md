# Claims, disclaimers, and approvals

Food and sourcing claims carry legal weight: certification labels are
regulated, and health claims about food are not allowed in our marketing. This
file is internal and is never shared with partners or the public.

## Required disclaimers

| When | Disclaimer text | Where it goes |
|------|-----------------|---------------|
| Fresh Drop signup | "Your subscription renews every two or four weeks until you cancel. Skip or cancel any time online." | Next to the signup button, and in the order confirmation |
| Free shipping offers | State exactly what qualifies, for example "Free shipping on Fresh Drop subscriptions." | Same line as the offer |
| Decaf coffees | "Decaf" only for coffees that are decaffeinated; never call a light roast "low caffeine" | Product page |

## Words and claims we cannot use

These are legal limits. Brand word choices belong in `voice/terms.yaml`
instead.

- **"Organic":** only for a coffee that is certified organic, with a current
  certificate on file, and only in the wording the certifier allows. Today, no
  Ridgeline coffee carries this claim.
- **"Fair Trade" or "Fair Trade Certified":** only for a coffee with current
  Fair Trade certification for that lot. Never use it as a general word for
  paying farmers well.
- **"Direct trade":** only when we have a written agreement with the named farm
  or co-op, and the product page names it.
- **Health claims:** none. No "boosts energy", "antioxidants", "good for your
  heart", "aids weight loss", or anything about caffeine and health.
- **Origin claims:** a coffee is named for a farm or region only when the lot
  comes from there; blends list every origin or none.

## Who approves

| Content | Approver |
|---------|----------|
| Press releases | Owner |
| Ads, product pages, and packaging | Owner and head roaster |
| Any sourcing or certification claim | Owner, with the certificate or agreement in hand |
| Subscription and shipping terms | Online shop manager and owner |
| Wholesale contracts | Owner and legal counsel |
