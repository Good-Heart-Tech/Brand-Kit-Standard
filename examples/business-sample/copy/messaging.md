# Approved messaging (internal)

This file is for staff only. It is **not** in the public press kit, because
realistic pricing, wholesale terms, and order wording make fake shop sites and
fake invoices easier to write.

## Tagline

**Roasted fresh. Poured with care.**

Short form for cups and stickers: **Fresh from the ridge.**

## One-sentence description

Ridgeline Coffee Roasters is a small-batch roaster with two cafes, an online
shop, and wholesale coffee for local businesses.

## Product descriptions

**Switchback (medium house blend):** Smooth and balanced, with notes of milk
chocolate, toasted almond, and a little cherry. Our everyday coffee, made for
drip, French press, and espresso with milk. 12 oz bag, $17.

**Summit (dark roast):** Bold and syrupy, with dark chocolate and molasses, but
never burnt. Stands up to cream and sugar. 12 oz bag, $17.

**First Light (light roast):** Bright and floral, with notes of peach, honey,
and black tea. Best as pour-over. 12 oz bag, $19.

**Fresh Drop subscription:** A bag of whatever we are most excited about,
roasted and shipped within 48 hours. Every two or four weeks. Skip or cancel
any time online. From $16 a bag, free shipping.

## Wholesale pitch

> Your guests notice when coffee is fresh. We roast to order every week,
> deliver on a set day, and lend and maintain brewing equipment for qualifying
> accounts. Every new partner gets on-site staff training, an espresso dial-in
> visit, and a direct line to our wholesale team.

## Wholesale terms (internal only)

- Pricing starts at $11.50 per pound for house blends and $14 per pound for
  single origins, with volume tiers above 40 pounds a week
- Net 30 terms after the first two orders; the first two orders are paid at
  delivery
- Equipment loans for accounts ordering 20 pounds or more a week
- Invoices come only from our accounting system, and **our bank details never
  change by email**. Any change is confirmed by a phone call to a number already
  on file, never a number in the message
