# Colors

Our colors come from the roastery: espresso, crema, copper kettles, and the
sage hills behind the ridge. The official values live in
`tokens/colors.bkr.json`. Open `tokens/exports/html/brand-at-a-glance.html` to
see them.

## Defaults

- Page background: `surface` (crema, a warm off-white)
- Headings: `ink` (espresso)
- Paragraphs and product details: `body`
- Shop, Add to cart, and Order wholesale buttons: `onPrimary` label on `copper`
- Links: `link` (a deeper copper that stays readable at small sizes)
- Product cards and tasting-note panels: `card`, with `body` text
- Illustrations, origin maps, and packaging patterns: `sage`
- Product photos and the reversed logo: `white`

## Dark mode

The dark theme is a "dark roast" look: `surface` becomes a deep coffee brown,
`ink` and `body` become warm milk tones, `link` brightens, and `card` becomes
mocha. `copper` buttons keep their white labels in both themes.

## Do not

- Use `sage` for text or button labels (it is too light)
- Put `copper` text on `ink` (it looks muddy and is hard to read)
- Fill whole pages or packaging with `copper`; it is for actions and accents
- Use pure `white` as a page background; use `surface` so the site feels warm
