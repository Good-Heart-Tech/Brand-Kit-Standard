# Typography

We pair a warm serif for headings with a clean sans for everything else. Both
are free on Google Fonts under the SIL Open Font License, and both are in
Canva's font list. Values live in `tokens/typography.bkr.json`.

- **Headings, menu boards, bag labels:** DM Serif Display (`heading` font),
  `regular` weight. It only comes in one weight, so never fake bold it.
- **Paragraphs, product details, prices, buttons:** DM Sans (`body` font),
  `regular` for text and `bold` for buttons, prices, and labels.
- **Sizes:** `base` for body text, `large` for tasting notes and lead
  paragraphs, `heading` for section titles, `display` for the homepage hero and
  cafe signage. Use `small` only for roast dates, badges, and fine print.

## Tips

- Keep headings short; DM Serif Display looks best at large sizes.
- Do not set long paragraphs in the serif; switch to DM Sans.
- On menu boards, use DM Sans `bold` for prices so they line up and read fast.
