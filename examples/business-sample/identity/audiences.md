# Who we talk to

| Audience | What they care about | How we sound to them |
|----------|----------------------|----------------------|
| Shoppers | How it tastes, how to brew it, freshness, and fast delivery | Inviting and concrete; tasting notes first |
| Cafe guests | A friendly welcome, a good cup, and a comfortable place to sit | Short, friendly, local |
| Fresh Drop subscribers | Fresh coffee on time and easy changes to their plan | Warm and useful, with a brew tip |
| Wholesale buyers (restaurants, offices, grocers) | Consistency, freshness, reliable delivery, and support | Professional and specific |
| Press (food writers, coffee media, business reporters) | What is new and facts they can check | Factual and quotable |
