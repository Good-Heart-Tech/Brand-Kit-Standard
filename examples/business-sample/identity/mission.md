# Mission, vision, and values

## Mission

We make really good coffee feel easy and welcoming, not exclusive: roasted
fresh from farms we know by name and served with care.

## Vision

A neighborhood where great coffee is something everyone enjoys, however they
take it.

## Values

- **Warm:** we treat everyone like a regular, even on their first visit.
- **Knowledgeable:** we know our farms, roasts, and brew methods, and we share freely.
- **Not snobby:** there is no wrong way to enjoy coffee, cream and sugar included.
- **Dependable:** fresh roast dates, orders that arrive when we said, and honest answers.
