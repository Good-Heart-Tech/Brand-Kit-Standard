# About Ridgeline Coffee Roasters

Ridgeline Coffee Roasters buys green coffee from farms we know by name, roasts
it in small batches, and gets it to people while it is still fresh. We sell
through two cafes, an online shop with home delivery and subscriptions, and
wholesale accounts with local restaurants, offices, and grocers.

We exist to make really good coffee feel easy and welcoming, not exclusive.

## Personality

- Warm: we talk to people like regulars, even on their first visit
- Knowledgeable: we know our farms, roast levels, and brew methods, and we share
  that freely
- Not snobby: there is no wrong way to enjoy your coffee, cream and sugar included
- Dependable: fresh roast dates, orders that arrive when we said, honest answers

## Who we talk to

- **Shoppers:** people buying beans, gear, or subscriptions online
- **Cafe guests:** neighbors, commuters, and remote workers at our two cafes
- **Wholesale buyers:** restaurant owners, office managers, and grocery buyers
  who care about consistency, freshness, and support
- **Press:** local food writers, coffee media, and business reporters
