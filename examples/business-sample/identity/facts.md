## Never claim

- "Organic", "Fair Trade", or "direct trade" for any coffee unless
  `copy/claims.md` allows it for that exact coffee
- Health benefits of any kind ("boosts energy", "full of antioxidants")
- "The best coffee in the world", "the best in town", or awards we have not won
- "Sustainable", "carbon neutral", or "zero waste" without a documented source
- Unannounced cafes, products, or gift bundles, including working names in
  `identity/naming.md`
- Sales numbers, the number of wholesale accounts, or wholesale prices in
  anything the public sees
