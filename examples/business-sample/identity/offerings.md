# What we offer

Prices live in `copy/messaging.md` (internal), because they change. Do not
quote prices from memory.

| Offering | What it is | Who it is for | Why choose it |
|----------|------------|---------------|---------------|
| Coffee | House blends (Switchback, Summit, First Light) and single origins named by farm or co-op | Shoppers and cafe guests | Small-batch roasts with the roast date on every bag |
| Fresh Drop | A coffee subscription, roasted and shipped within 48 hours, every two or four weeks | Home coffee drinkers | Always fresh; skip or cancel any time online |
| Online shop | Beans, brewing gear, and subscriptions, shipped nationwide | Shoppers | Fresh coffee delivered to your door |
| Cafes | Ridgeline Downtown and Ridgeline North End | Neighbors, commuters, and remote workers | Friendly baristas and order-ahead in the app |
| Ridgeline Wholesale | Coffee roasted to order every week, delivered on a set day, with staff training and an espresso dial-in visit | Restaurants, offices, and grocers | Consistent, fresh coffee and a direct line to our wholesale team |

## What we do not offer

- Locations other than Ridgeline Downtown and Ridgeline North End. Do not
  mention or hint at new locations until they are announced.
- Shipping outside the country.
- Private-label or co-branded coffee without a written agreement.
- Franchises or licensing of the Ridgeline name.
- Health or nutrition benefits of any kind (see `copy/claims.md`).
- Payment by gift card, wire transfer, or any link sent by text or email that
  did not come from our online shop or accounting system.
