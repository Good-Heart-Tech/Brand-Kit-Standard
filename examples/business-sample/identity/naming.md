# Naming

- **Legal name:** Ridgeline Coffee Roasters, LLC
- **Everyday name:** Ridgeline Coffee Roasters
- **Short name (after first mention):** Ridgeline
- **Cafes:** "Ridgeline Downtown" and "Ridgeline North End"
- **Programs:** "Ridgeline Wholesale" (accounts for businesses) and "Fresh Drop"
  (our coffee subscription)
- **Coffees:** origin first, then farm or co-op, for example "Huila, Finca
  La Esperanza". House blends get a short trail name: "Switchback" (medium),
  "Summit" (dark), "First Light" (light).

## Do not

- Write "RCR" or "Ridgeline Coffee" in anything customers see
- Add "LLC" outside contracts, invoices, and legal pages
- Name a blend after a real trail without checking it is not someone's trademark
