# Who we talk to

| Audience | What they care about | How we sound to them |
|----------|----------------------|----------------------|
| Residents | What to do, by when, where, and what it costs | Plain, direct, step by step; 6th to 8th grade reading level |
| Residents with low vision, older adults, and people with disabilities | Information they can read, hear, and use | Large, clear text; captions; accommodations offered up front |
| Spanish speakers and people who read English as a second language | Key information in their language | Short sentences that translate well; Spanish for alerts and key pages |
| Local businesses | Permits, licenses, inspections, and timelines | Professional, specific, no jargon |
| Other agencies (cities, school districts, the state, neighboring counties) | Coordination and accurate shared information | Formal and precise |
| Reporters and local media | Facts, dates, and who to quote | Factual and neutral; officials quoted by title |
