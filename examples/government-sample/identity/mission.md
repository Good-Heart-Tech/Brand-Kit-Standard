# Mission, vision, and values

## Mission

Brightwater County provides the everyday services that keep the Brightwater
Valley safe, healthy, and connected, for every resident.

## Vision

Every resident can find, understand, and use county services the first time
they try.

## Values

- **Helpful:** we lead with what people need to do and how to do it.
- **Neutral:** we inform; we do not persuade or take sides.
- **Trustworthy:** our information is accurate, consistent, and easy to verify.
- **Accessible:** our services work for everyone, including people with disabilities and people who speak limited English.
- **Calm:** we stay steady in emergencies and never sound alarmist.
