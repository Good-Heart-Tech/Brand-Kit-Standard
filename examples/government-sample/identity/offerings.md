# What we offer

County services, named for what they do. Fees and deadlines change, so link to
the service page on brightwatercounty.example instead of quoting them.

| Service | What it is | Who it is for | Why it matters |
|---------|------------|---------------|----------------|
| Parks and trails | County parks, trails, and trail conditions, run by Brightwater County Parks | Residents and visitors | Free places to get outside across the valley |
| Public health clinics | Free Flu Shot Clinics and other clinics run by Brightwater County Public Health | Residents | Keeps the community healthy |
| Elections | Voter registration, Vote by Mail, polling places, and results, run by Brightwater County Elections | Registered and eligible voters | Fair, accurate, and accessible elections |
| Property records | Deeds, property records, and maps | Property owners, buyers, and businesses | Official records people can rely on |
| Roads | County road maintenance, closures, and repairs | Drivers and residents | Safe travel across the valley |
| Permits and licenses | Building permits, business licenses, and dog licenses | Residents and local businesses | Apply and renew online or at any county office |
| Emergency alerts | Official alerts and updates during emergencies | Everyone in the county | Timely, accurate safety information |

## What we do not offer

- Schools. School districts run them.
- State services, such as driver's licenses and state taxes.
- City services inside city limits, unless the city and County have an
  agreement. Point people to their city when unsure.
- Legal, tax, or medical advice for a person's own situation.
- Endorsements of any business, product, candidate, or cause.
- Payment by text message, gift card, or wire transfer, ever.
