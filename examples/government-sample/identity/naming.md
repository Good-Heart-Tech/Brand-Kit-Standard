# Naming

- **Legal name:** Brightwater County
- **Everyday name:** Brightwater County
- **After first mention:** the County (capital C when it means the government)
- **Governing body:** the Brightwater County Board of Commissioners

## Departments

Departments always lead with the county name, then a plain description of what
they do. They do not get their own logos, colors, or taglines.

| Write this | Not this |
|------------|----------|
| Brightwater County Parks | BC Parks, Brightwater Parks & Rec Dept. |
| Brightwater County Public Health | BCPH, the Health District |
| Brightwater County Elections | the Elections Office (on first mention) |

After first mention, a short form is fine: "Parks," "Public Health,"
"Elections."

## Programs and services

Name a service for what it does: "Free Flu Shot Clinics," "Trail Conditions,"
"Vote by Mail." Avoid clever or branded program names that residents have to
learn.

## Do not

- Use "BWC" or other initials in anything residents see
- Abbreviate "County" as "Co." in headings or signs
- Put a person's name (such as a commissioner or director) in a department
  name or logo; officials change, the department does not
- Create a separate logo for a department, program, or event
