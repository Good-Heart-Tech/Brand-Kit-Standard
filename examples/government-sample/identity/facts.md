## Never claim

- That the County supports or opposes any candidate, party, or ballot measure
- That an area is "safe" or an emergency is "over" before the responsible
  department confirms it
- Numbers (case counts, budget figures, turnout, crime rates) that do not come
  from the responsible department's published data
- That a permit, license, or application is "guaranteed" or will be approved
- That the County endorses a business, product, or event
- That a page, account, or document is "official" unless the County issued it
