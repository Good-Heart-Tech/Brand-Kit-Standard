# Claims, disclaimers, and approvals

County words carry legal weight: public notices are required by law, and
accessibility and nondiscrimination statements are legal commitments. This
file is internal and is never shared with partners or the public. Like
everything in this kit, it may be a public record.

## Required statements

| When | Text | Where it goes |
|------|------|---------------|
| Public notices and meeting pages | Accessibility line from `copy/messaging.md`: "To ask for an accommodation, such as an interpreter, large print, or captions, visit brightwatercounty.example/accessibility at least 3 business days before the event." | End of every notice and event page |
| Every program and service page | "Brightwater County does not discriminate on the basis of race, color, national origin, disability, age, or sex in its programs and services." | Page footer |
| Bills, notices, and service pages | Payment safety line from `copy/messaging.md` | Near any payment instructions |
| Every notice, letter, and post | Official website line from `copy/messaging.md` | Footer or last line |
| Websites | Link to the County accessibility statement, with a way to report a barrier | Every page footer |

## Public notices

- A **Notice** is an official public notice required by law. Its required
  parts (date, time, place, subject, and legal authority) and publication
  deadlines come from the County Attorney's checklist. Never use the word
  "Notice" for ordinary announcements.
- Do not rewrite the legal text of an ordinance, resolution, or notice for
  style. Add a plain-language summary above it, labeled "Summary", and keep
  the official text unchanged.
- Meeting times in a notice are official. If a time changes, publish a new
  notice; do not edit the old one without marking it "Updated" with the date.

## Words and claims we cannot use

These are legal limits. Plain-language word choices belong in
`voice/terms.yaml` instead.

- **"Official":** only for county-issued documents, websites, and accounts.
- **"Free":** only when the service has no cost to anyone who qualifies.
- **"Guaranteed" or "approved":** never about a permit, license, or benefit
  before the decision is made.
- **Campaign words:** never "vote yes", "vote no", "support", or "oppose" in
  any county material about a ballot measure or candidate.
- **The seal:** never on anything that is not an official act of the County
  (see `copy/legal.md`).

## Who approves

| Content | Approver |
|---------|----------|
| Public notices, ordinances, and resolutions | County Clerk and County Attorney |
| Press releases | Communications Office |
| Election information | Elections Director |
| Emergency alerts | Emergency Management Director |
| Accessibility statements and accommodation wording | ADA Coordinator |
| Anything about lawsuits or personnel | County Attorney |
