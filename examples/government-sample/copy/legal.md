# Legal

## The county seal

<img src="../assets/seal/county-seal.svg" alt="Official Seal of Brightwater County" width="160">

The Official Seal of Brightwater County (`assets/seal/county-seal.svg`) is a
protected mark. It shows that a document is an official act of the County.

- Only county departments may use the seal, and only on official documents and
  property: ordinances, resolutions, certificates, official letters, signed
  notices, badges, and county vehicles and buildings.
- The seal is **never** given to partners, vendors, or the press, and it is
  not in the partner bundle (`bkr publish`). Partners and press use the county
  logo instead.
- The seal lives in `assets/seal/`, not `assets/logo/`, so the generated
  brand-at-a-glance page (which embeds every file in `assets/logo/` and is
  shared with partners) never contains it.
- Do not use the seal on promotional items, social media graphics, event
  flyers, or anything that is not an official act of the County.
- Do not change, recolor, crop, or combine the seal with other marks.
- Use of the seal to make something look like official county business, when
  it is not, will be reported to law enforcement.

## The county logo

- The Brightwater County name and logo identify the County. Partners and the
  press may use the files in the partner bundle to refer to the County or to a
  joint program the County has approved.
- Do not use the logo in a way that suggests the County endorses a business,
  product, candidate, or cause.

## Public records

Everything in this kit, including internal drafts and naming notes, may be a
public record under state public records law. Write as if any page could be
released. Never put personal information, passwords, or security details in
this kit.

## Reporting impersonation

If you see a website, text message, email, or social media account pretending
to be Brightwater County, report it at
https://brightwatercounty.example/report-fraud.
