# Style rules

A short checklist, not a style manual. When something is not covered here,
follow the federal plain language guidelines, then AP style, and add the
decision here.

- **Reading level:** 6th to 8th grade for service pages, alerts, and social media
- **Headings:** Sentence case ("Renew your dog license"); official service names keep Title Case ("Free Flu Shot Clinics")
- **Our name:** "Brightwater County" on first mention, then "the County" (capital C when it means the government)
- **Departments:** County name first: "Brightwater County Public Health", then "Public Health"
- **Officials:** Quote by title: "Elections Director", not a first name alone
- **Dates and times:** In full: "Tuesday, November 4, at 6 p.m."; "2026-11-04" only in data and file names
- **Numbers:** Spell out one to nine; numerals for 10 and up, money, and percentages; "1 minute" in alerts so it stands out
- **Lists:** Oxford comma; use bulleted steps for anything with more than two steps
- **Person:** "we" for the County, "you" for the reader
- **Acronyms:** Spell out on first use: "Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA)"
- **Forms:** Say what the form is for, then the number: "Building Permit Application (Form BP-1)"
- **Links:** Underlined, and the text says where it goes; never "click here"
- **Exclamation points and emoji:** None in alerts, notices, election information, or public health information
- **Spanish:** Emergency alerts and key service pages are translated, not machine-translated without review
