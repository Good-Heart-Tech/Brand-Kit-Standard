# Sensitive topics

How we talk about subjects where a careless sentence could hurt trust. For each
topic: our position, what to say, what not to say, and who approves anything
public. If a topic says **no comment**, do not write about it at all.

| Topic | Our position | Say | Do not say | Approver |
|-------|--------------|-----|------------|----------|
| Elections | Strictly neutral and factual | How, when, and where to register and vote; deadlines; how ballots are counted; official results when certified | Anything that suggests how to vote; unofficial results as final; guesses about turnout | Elections Director |
| Ballot measures | Neutral facts only; public resources are never used to campaign | The official ballot title, the election date, and where to read the full text | "Vote yes", "vote no", "support", or "oppose" | County Attorney and Elections Director |
| Emergencies | Calm, urgent, action first | What is happening, who is affected, what to do now, and when the next update comes | Guesses, rumors, or "all clear" before the responsible department confirms it | Emergency Management Director and Communications Office |
| Public health | Follow the science and the Public Health Officer | What residents should do, where to get care, and where to learn more | Case numbers or advice that Public Health has not published | Public Health Officer |
| Politics and candidates | No comment | | Any position on candidates, parties, or elected officials' campaigns | County Administrator |
| Lawsuits and personnel matters | No comment | "The County does not comment on pending legal or personnel matters." | Any details | County Attorney |
| Fees and taxes | Only what the Board has approved | The approved change, when it starts, and where to read the decision | Proposed numbers as if they are final | Finance Director and Communications Office |
