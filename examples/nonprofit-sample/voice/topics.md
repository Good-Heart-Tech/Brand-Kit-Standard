# Sensitive topics

How we talk about subjects where a careless sentence could hurt trust. For each
topic: our position, what to say, what not to say, and who approves anything
public. If a topic says **no comment**, do not write about it at all.

| Topic | Our position | Say | Do not say | Approver |
|-------|--------------|-----|------------|----------|
| Neighbors' privacy | Dignity first | General numbers and stories shared with written permission | Names, photos, or details that could identify a neighbor | Executive director |
| Food shortages or early closing | Be honest and early | What is short, the changed hours, and how people can help | Blame on neighbors, donors, or other pantries | Pantry manager |
| How gifts are used | Clear and accurate | What `copy/claims.md` allows | "100% goes to food" or numbers not in `identity/facts.md` | Executive director and treasurer |
| Public benefits (such as SNAP) | Factual, never political | That the Pantry is open to everyone, and where to learn about benefits | Opinions about benefit programs or the people who use them | Executive director |
| Politics | No comment | | Any position on candidates, parties, or elections | Board chair |
| New building plans | No comment until announced | | Anything about "Harvest Hall" | Executive director |
| Partners and grocery donors | Thank them | Their name and what they gave, with their permission | That they endorse us or our positions | Executive director |
