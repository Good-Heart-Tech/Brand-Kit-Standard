## Identity (excerpt)

# About Cedar Hollow Community Pantry

Cedar Hollow Community Pantry gives free groceries to any neighbor who needs
them, no questions asked. About 120 volunteers keep our shelves stocked and
our doors open four days a week.

## Personality

- Welcoming, never pitying
- Neighborly and down to earth
- Grateful
- Practical: we talk about food, hours, and how to help

## Who we talk to

- Neighbors who use the pantry (many read English as a second language)
- Volunteers, including students and retirees
- Individual donors and local businesses
- Grant funders and the county food coalition

## Voice (excerpt)

# Voice and tone

We sound like a friendly neighbor: warm, plain, and respectful.

| Situation | Tone | Example |
|-----------|------|---------|
| Signs and flyers for neighbors | Welcoming, simple | "Free groceries. Everyone welcome. No ID needed." |
| Volunteer recruiting | Upbeat, specific | "Two hours on a Saturday packs 60 backpacks." |
| Donation asks | Grateful, concrete | "$25 fills a family's grocery bag for a week." |
| Social media | Warm, hopeful | "Thanks to you, 340 families had fresh produce this month." |
| Grant reports | Professional, factual | Lead with numbers served and outcomes. |

## Always

- Aim for a 6th to 8th grade reading level
- Put the most important information first (hours, address, what to bring)
- Use photos only with written permission from the people in them

## Vocabulary highlights

- "neighbors" over "clients", "recipients", or "the needy"
- "groceries" or "food" over "food assistance"
- "volunteer" over "resource"
- "pantry hours" over "distribution schedule"
- "Handout", "charity case", "less fortunate"
- Guilt in donation appeals ("Imagine a child going hungry...")
- Acronyms like "SNAP" without explaining them
- Gender-neutral defaults
- Short sentences that translate well

# Approved messaging (internal)

This file is for staff and volunteers. It is **not** in the public press kit,
because realistic donation wording makes fake donation appeals easier to write.

**Volunteer ask:** Two hours on a Saturday packs 60 weekend backpacks for

**For neighbors:** Free groceries. Everyone welcome. No ID needed.
Tuesday to Friday, 10am to 6pm.
