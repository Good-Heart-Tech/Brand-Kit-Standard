# Approved facts

Last reviewed: 2026-10-02

People and AI tools may state these facts as written. Anything not listed here
needs a source before it is published. Update the date above whenever you
review this page; `obks validate` warns when it is more than a year old.

| Fact | Source or owner |
|------|-----------------|
| Cedar Hollow Community Pantry was founded in 2009 | Press kit (`copy/press-kit.md`); executive director |
| The Pantry serves about 340 families each month | Monthly sign-in counts; pantry manager |
| About 120 volunteers keep the Pantry running | Volunteer roster; volunteer coordinator |
| The Pantry is open four days a week, Tuesday to Friday, 10am to 6pm | Pantry manager |
| Groceries are free, and no ID is needed | Pantry policy; executive director |
| $25 fills a family's grocery bag for a week | Food cost estimate, reviewed each year; treasurer |
| Two hours on a Saturday packs 60 weekend backpacks | Weekend Backpacks program records; volunteer coordinator |

## Never claim

- "We are ending hunger" or "no one in Cedar Hollow goes hungry"
- "100% of your gift goes to food" or "every dollar buys food"
- Meal counts, pounds of food, or other numbers not in this table
- That a business, grocer, or funder endorses the Pantry, unless they approved
  it in writing
- Anything about "Harvest Hall" or a new building until it is announced
- Names, photos, or personal stories of neighbors without written permission
