# Mission, vision, and values

## Mission

Cedar Hollow Community Pantry gives free groceries to any neighbor who needs
them, no questions asked.

## Vision

A Cedar Hollow where every neighbor has enough good food.

## Values

- **Welcoming:** everyone is welcome, with no ID needed and no pity.
- **Neighborly:** we are neighbors helping neighbors, and we sound like it.
- **Grateful:** volunteers and donors make every bag of groceries possible, and we say so.
- **Practical:** we talk about food, hours, and how to help.
