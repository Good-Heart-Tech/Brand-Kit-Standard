# Who we talk to

| Audience | What they care about | How we sound to them |
|----------|----------------------|----------------------|
| Neighbors who use the Pantry | Hours, address, what to bring, and that they are welcome | Welcoming and simple; short sentences that translate well |
| Volunteers (including students and retirees) | When to come, what they will do, and the difference it makes | Upbeat and specific |
| Individual donors | That their gift helps, and how | Grateful and concrete, never guilt |
| Local businesses | Simple ways to give or sponsor, and community goodwill | Friendly and professional |
| Grant funders and the county food coalition | Numbers served, outcomes, and responsible use of funds | Professional and factual |
| Press | A clear story and facts they can check | Factual; neighbors' privacy comes first |
