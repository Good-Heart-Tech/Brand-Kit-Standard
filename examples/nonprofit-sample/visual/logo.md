# Logo

| File | Use |
|------|-----|
| `assets/logo/mark.svg` | Default, on light backgrounds |
| `assets/logo/mark-reversed.svg` | On green or dark photos |
| `assets/logo/wordmark.svg` | Flyers, website header, letterhead |

## Clear space

Leave space around the logo at least as wide as the tree trunk in the mark.

## Do not

- Change the colors
- Stretch or rotate it
- Add other organizations' logos inside our clear space without approval
