# Claims, disclaimers, and approvals

Fundraising wording carries legal weight: donors rely on it for their taxes,
and states regulate charitable appeals. This file is internal and is never
shared with partners or the public.

## Required disclaimers

| When | Disclaimer text | Where it goes |
|------|-----------------|---------------|
| Donation pages and appeals | "Cedar Hollow Community Pantry, Inc. is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit. Gifts are tax-deductible to the extent allowed by law." | Same page as the giving button |
| Donation receipts | "No goods or services were provided in exchange for this gift." (or a description and fair value of anything given, such as an event meal) | Every receipt |
| Event tickets and auctions | "The tax-deductible amount is the ticket price minus the value of goods and services received, [amount]." | On the ticket page and receipt |
| Any "$25 fills a bag" style example | Keep the example exactly as approved in `identity/facts.md`, and do not promise that a gift buys one specific item | Wherever the example appears |

## How gifts are used

- Unrestricted gifts support the Pantry's work: food, the building, and the
  costs of keeping the doors open. Say "Your gift keeps the Pantry stocked and
  open", not "Your gift buys food" alone.
- A gift for a named program (for example Weekend Backpacks) is used only for
  that program. Do not offer this choice unless the treasurer can track it.
- Never say "100% of your gift goes to food" or "no overhead".

## Words and claims we cannot use

These are legal limits. Brand word choices belong in `voice/terms.yaml`
instead.

- "Tax-deductible" without "to the extent allowed by law"
- "Matching gift" or "your gift will be doubled" unless a sponsor has
  committed the match in writing, with its limit and deadline
- "Government-funded" or "government-approved"
- Any claim that a donation is needed "today or a family goes hungry"
  (urgency must be true and specific)

## Who approves

| Content | Approver |
|---------|----------|
| Donation pages, appeals, and receipts | Executive director and treasurer |
| Grant applications and reports | Executive director |
| Press releases | Executive director |
| Anything with numbers or outcomes | Executive director, checked against `identity/facts.md` |
| Sponsor and matching-gift wording | Board treasurer |
