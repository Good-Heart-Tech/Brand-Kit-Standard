## Clear space

Leave empty space around the logo equal to at least 12% of its width.

## Colorways

- Default: full-color mark on `surface`
- On dark backgrounds: white (reversed) version on `ink`

## Do not

- Stretch, rotate, or add shadows
- Combine the program mark with partner logos without approval
