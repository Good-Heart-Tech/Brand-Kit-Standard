## Clear space

Leave empty space around the logo equal to at least 12% of its width.

## Colorways

- Default: full-color logo on white
- On dark backgrounds: use a white (reversed) version on `ink`

## Do not

- Stretch, rotate, or add shadows
- Change the logo colors
- Place the logo on busy photos
