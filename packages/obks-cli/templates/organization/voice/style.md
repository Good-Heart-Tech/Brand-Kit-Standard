# Style rules

A short checklist, not a style manual. When something is not covered here,
follow a common guide (for example AP or Chicago) and add the decision here.

> TODO(obks): Confirm or change each rule.

- **Headings:** Sentence case ("Book a free call", not "Book A Free Call")
- **Lists:** Oxford comma ("red, white, and blue")
- **Numbers:** Spell out one to nine; use numerals for 10 and up, money, and percentages
- **Dates:** "October 2, 2026" in prose; "2026-10-02" in data and file names
- **Times:** "9 a.m." and "2:30 p.m."
- **Person:** "we" for the organization, "you" for the reader
- **Exclamation points:** At most one per page
- **Emoji:** Only on social media, never in headings or legal text
- **Links:** Describe where the link goes ("see pricing"), never "click here"
