# Vocabulary

## Prefer

- "you" over "the user" or "the customer" when speaking to people directly
- "team" over "resource"
- "email" over "e-mail"

## Avoid

- Jargon and acronyms without explanation
- Words that shame, label, or talk down to people
- Hype words like "revolutionary" or "best-in-class"
